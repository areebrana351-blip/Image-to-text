What Is Image to Text AI?

Image to Text AI is an online OCR tool that helps you extract written or printed text from images. Instead of typing text manually, you can upload an image and let the OCR technology recognize the text for you.

Why Use an Image to Text Converter?

An image to text converter can be useful when you need to copy information from screenshots, scanned documents, notes, photographs, receipts or other images. OCR can save time by turning visible text into editable content.
